## Purpose

To foster an open, professional, and safe collaboration environment for everyone contributing to ClimateShield Nexus, including government staff, contracted developers, researchers, and community contributors.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing for mistakes

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional government-technology setting

## Government & Public-Sector Context

Because ClimateShield Nexus processes data relevant to national disaster response and public safety, contributors must additionally:

- Never fabricate or misrepresent climate data, model outputs, or test results.
- Disclose conflicts of interest relevant to procurement or vendor relationships.
- Treat all disaster/casualty-related data referenced in issues or PRs as sensitive (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issue tracker, chat channels, meetings) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement Process

1. Report incidents to the project governance contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
2. Maintainers review within 5 business days.
3. Outcomes range from a private warning to removal from the project, depending on severity.

## Enforcement Guidelines

| Level | Description | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | Minor, first-time issue | Private written warning |
| 2. Warning | A single incident or series of actions | Warning with consequences for continued behavior |
| 3. Temporary Ban | Serious or sustained violation | Temporary removal from project spaces |
| 4. Permanent Ban | Pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression | Permanent removal |
